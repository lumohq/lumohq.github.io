What Tidy does

Tidy suggests photos, videos and files you may want to remove. Suggestions are automatic and can be wrong. You decide what to delete and you confirm every deletion. Review each item first. Items removed from your photo library go to Recently Deleted, and space is freed when that album is emptied or its retention period ends.
