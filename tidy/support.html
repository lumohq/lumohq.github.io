Tidy is made by Lumo. Open an issue on GitHub and include your iOS version and what you were doing.

Common questions

Where did my deleted photos go?

Photos you confirm go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app. Storage is freed when that album is emptied or after its retention period ends.

Tidy cannot see all my photos.

You chose Limited access. Change it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Tidy. With Limited access, duplicates outside your selection are missed.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

Does Tidy upload my photos?

No. Analysis happens on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
