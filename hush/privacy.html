Hush

Privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026

In short: Hush collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Lumo, the maker of Hush, never receives your browsing history or any other personal information.

How blocking works

Hush gives Safari a list of rules. Safari applies them on your device. Hush itself never sees which websites you visit, and nothing about your browsing is sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your choices (which filters are on, your allowed websites and custom rules) and the downloaded filter lists are stored only on your device, inside Hush. Deleting the app deletes them.

Filter list downloads

To stay up to date, Hush downloads public filter lists directly from their publishers:

  • AdGuard (filters.adtidy.org)
  • Peter Lowe's list (pgl.yoyo.org)
  • Malicious URL blocklist (malware-filter.gitlab.io), if you turn on the phishing filter

Like any website, these servers can see your IP address when a list is downloaded. The request contains nothing about you or your browsing. Their own privacy policies apply.

YouTube

The optional "Hush for YouTube" extension runs only on youtube.com, inside Safari, to hide and skip ads. It does not record or send anything.

Purchases

If Hush offers in-app purchases, Apple processes them. Lumo receives no payment details.

Children

Hush collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Its history is public in this site's repository.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
