Support

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Ask for help on GitHub.

Hush isn't blocking anything

Safari extensions have to be switched on in Settings. Open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions (on iOS 17: Settings › Safari › Extensions) and turn on every "Hush" blocker. Then open Hush once so it can load its filters.

YouTube ads still play

In Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Hush for YouTube, turn the extension on and set youtube.com to Allow. This works in Safari only, not in the YouTube app. YouTube changes often; if ads come back, update Hush.

A website looks broken

In Hush, open Allowed websites and add the site. Nothing is blocked there. You can also tap aA in Safari's address bar, then Turn off Content Blockers for a quick check.

Does Hush work in Chrome or other apps?

No. iOS lets ad blockers work only in Safari, and in apps that open web pages with Safari.

Does Hush see my browsing?

No. Safari applies Hush's rules on your device; Hush collects no data. See the privacy policy.

Report a problem or suggest a feature

Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iOS version and, for a website problem, its address.
