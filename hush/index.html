Hush

The internet. Without the noise.

Hush is a Safari ad blocker for iPhone and iPad, made by Lumo.

  • Blocks ads and the trackers that follow you between sites.
  • Hides cookie notices, pop-ups and "open in app" banners.
  • Skips YouTube video ads in Safari.
  • Pages load faster and use less data, because blocked content is never downloaded.
  • Collects no data. Safari does the blocking; Hush never sees what you browse.
