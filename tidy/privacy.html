Tidy

Privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026

In short: your photos, videos and files never leave your iPhone. Tidy has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and Lumo, the maker of Tidy, collects no personal data.

What Tidy accesses on your device

  • Photo library. With your permission, Tidy reads your photos and videos to compare them on your device. When you confirm, it moves the items you selected to Recently Deleted. You may choose Limited access and share only some photos.
  • A folder you pick. The Files Cleaner reads only the folder you choose and remembers that choice on your device.
  • Storage figures. Tidy reads your total and free storage to show how much space you can recover.

What stays on your device

Your onboarding answers, cleanup statistics, scan results, thumbnails and comparison data are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Crash reports

If you share app analytics with developers in iOS settings, Apple may provide Lumo with anonymous crash and performance reports.

What Lumo does not do

  • Sell or share your personal data.
  • Track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Use advertising SDKs.

Purchases

If Tidy offers a subscription, Apple processes the payment. Lumo receives no payment details.

Children

Tidy is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

Change photo access any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Tidy. Delete the app to remove all data stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date. Its history is public in this site's repository.

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
